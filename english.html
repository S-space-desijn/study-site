  • 指示代词：this/these（近），that/those（远）；电话用语 This is… speaking. Is that…?

名词复数变化

  • 一般加 -s：books；以 s/x/ch/sh 结尾加 -es：buses, boxes, watches；辅音字母+y 变 y 为 i 加 -es：city→cities；以 o 结尾：potatoes, tomatoes（有生命的加 es），photos, pianos。
  • 以 f/fe 结尾变 ves：knife→knives, leaf→leaves, wife, half, wolf, thief, shelf。
  • 不规则：man→men, woman→women, child→children, foot→feet, tooth→teeth, mouse→mice, sheep→sheep, fish→fish, Chinese→Chinese。
  • 不可数名词：water, bread, milk, news, information, homework, advice, furniture；用量词：a bottle of water, two pieces of advice。
there be（某地存在某物）：There is + 单数/不可数；There are + 复数；就近原则：There is a pen and two books on the desk.
have got = have（某人拥有）：I have got a bike. = I have a bike. 否定 haven't got / 疑问 Have you got…?
there be 与 have 不能同时使用：✘ There has a book. → ✔ There is a book.
  • can（能、会、可以）：后接动词原形，无人称数变化；否定 can't；疑问 Can you…? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

I can swim, but I can't play the piano. Can you speak English?

我会游泳，但不会弹钢琴。你会说英语吗？

  • 祈使句：动词原形开头，否定 Don't + 动词原形：Open your books. Don't eat in class.
  • 现在进行时：am/is/are + doing；表示此刻或现阶段正在进行的动作；标志词 now, look, listen, at the moment, these days。
  • v-ing 变化：直接加 ing；去 e 加 ing（make→making）；重读闭音节双写末尾辅音（run→running, swim→swimming, sit→sitting）；ie 变 y 加 ing（lie→lying）。
某些动词一般不用进行时：know, like, love, want, have（拥有）, understand, believe。

七年级下册Modules 1–12

  • 名词性物主代词 = 形容词性物主代词 + 名词：mine = my+名词，其后不再跟名词。This bag is hers. = This is her bag.
  • 方位介词：in, on, under, next to（挨着）, behind, in front of（外部前面）, in the front of（内部前部）, between…and…, opposite（在……对面）。
  • could：can 的过去式（过去的能力）；也可表示现在更委婉的请求：Could you help me?

The bank is next to the hospital, opposite the station. Whose gloves are these? — They're mine.

银行在医院旁边、车站对面。这是谁的手套？——是我的。

① be going to + 动词原形：表示计划、打算，或根据迹象预测。I'm going to check my email.
② will + 动词原形：表示将来的事实、临时决定或无依据的预测。will not = won't。
③ 现在进行时可表将来：表示已安排好的动作 I'm leaving tomorrow.
  • 标志词：tomorrow, next week, in the future, soon, this evening, the day after tomorrow。
  • there be 句型的将来时：There is/are going to be…；There will be…（be 不能换成 have）。

— Will schools be different in the future? — Yes, they will. Students won't use pens and paper.

——将来学校会不一样吗？——会的。学生们将不再使用笔和纸。

表示过去某个时间发生的动作或存在的状态。
肯定：主语 + 动词过去式（V-ed / 不规则）；否定：didn't + 动词原形；疑问：Did + 主语 + 动词原形？
be 动词：was（I/he/she/it 单数）/ were（we/you/they 复数）。
  • 规则动词加 -ed：worked；以 e 结尾加 d：lived；辅音+y 变 i 加 ed：study→studied；重读闭音节双写：stop→stopped。
  • 标志词：yesterday, last night/week/year, …ago, in 2010, just now, the day before yesterday。
  • 高频不规则：go→went, come→came, see→saw, buy→bought, have→had, do→did, get→got, give→gave, take→took, eat→ate, run→ran, swim→swam, write→wrote, read→read（读音 /red/）, leave→left, find→found, tell→told, speak→spoke, spend→spent。
didn't / did 后动词必须还原：✘ Did you went? → ✔ Did you go?

  • 副词：修饰动词、形容词或其他副词；形容词 + ly 构成（quick→quickly, careful→carefully；辅音+y 变 i：happy→happily）；不规则：good→well, fast→fast, early→early, late→late。

  • 使役动词 make/let/have + 宾语 + do（不带 to）；help sb (to) do。
  • 过去进行时：was/were + doing；表示过去某一时刻或某段时间正在进行的动作；标志 at 8 o'clock last night, this time yesterday, from 7 to 9。

I was reading a book when my mother came back. While she was cooking, I was doing my homework.

妈妈回来时我正在看书。她做饭时我在写作业。（when 后接短暂动作用一般过去时；while 后接延续动作常用进行时）

八年级下册Modules 1–10

  • 系动词后接形容词作表语：be, look, sound, smell, taste, feel, seem, become, get, turn, keep, stay。

The soup smells delicious but tastes a little salty. Your idea sounds great. Silk feels soft.

  • 五种简单句：① 主+谓（S+V）Birds fly. ② 主+系+表 She is happy. ③ 主+谓+宾 I like apples. ④ 主+谓+间宾+直宾 He gave me a book. ⑤ 主+谓+宾+宾补 We keep our classroom clean.（常作宾补：形容词、名词、do/doing、介词短语）
结构：have/has + 过去分词（done）。
用法：① 过去发生的动作对现在造成影响或结果（常与 already, just, yet, ever, never 连用）；② 过去开始持续到现在的动作或状态（常与 since, for, so far, in the past/last…years 连用）。
  • already（肯定句中，have/has 后）；just（刚刚）；yet（否定句、疑问句末）；ever（曾经，疑问句）；never（从不，本身否定）。
  • 规则过去分词同过去式（-ed）；常见不规则：go—went—gone；be—was/were—been；see—saw—seen；do—did—done；have—had—had；give—gave—given；take—took—taken；write—wrote—written；eat—ate—eaten；buy—bought—bought。

Have you ever entered a competition? I've just finished my homework. She hasn't come back yet.

for + 时间段（for three years）；since + 时间点/一般过去时从句（since 2015, since I came here）。
have been to 去过（已回）；have gone to 去了（未回）；have been in 待在某地（持续）。
  • 延续性问题：非延续性动词（buy, borrow, die, leave, join, marry, begin, open, come, go）在完成时中不能与 for/since 连用，要转换：buy→have；borrow→keep；die→be dead；leave→be away；join→be a member of / be in；begin→be on；marry→be married；come/go→be in/at。

He has been away from his hometown for ten years.（不用 has left）I have had the bike since last month.（不用 have bought）

一般过去时与现在完成时区别：有明确过去时间（yesterday, last year, in 2008, …ago）用一般过去时；强调与现在的联系用完成时。
  • 宾语从句在复合句中作主句谓语动词（或介词、形容词）的宾语。
  • 引导词：① that（陈述句变来，口语常省略）：I think (that) he is right. ② if/whether（一般疑问句变来，“是否”）：I wonder if/whether he will come. ③ 连接代词/副词 what, who, when, where, why, how, which：Do you know where he lives?
  • 语序：一律用陈述语序（主语 + 谓语）。
  • 时态呼应：主句是现在时/将来时，从句按需要用时态；主句是过去时，从句用相应的过去时态；客观真理仍用一般现在时。

Could you tell me how I can get to the station? The teacher told us that light travels faster than sound.

could 在此表委婉，并非过去时；“光比声音快”是客观真理，从句用一般现在时。

九年级上册Modules 1–10（中考重点语法）

详见“语法与写作专题”中的八大时态总表。九上模块高频出现一般现在时、现在进行时、一般过去时、过去进行时、一般将来时、现在完成时的综合比较。

I have lived here since I was born. When I got home, my parents were watching TV. People today live longer than they did in the past.

基本结构：be + 过去分词（done）；be 动词随时态、人称数变化。
一般现在时：am/is/are + done；一般过去时：was/were + done；
一般将来时：will be + done / be going to be + done；
现在完成时：have/has been + done；
含情态动词：情态动词 + be + done。
  • 用法：不知道动作执行者、没有必要指出、强调动作承受者时用被动；动作执行者由 by 引出。

The Great Wall is visited by millions of people every year. The bridge was built in 1960. The work must be finished today. His new book has been translated into many languages.

主动变被动步骤：宾语提前作主语 → 谓语改 be+done（时态不变）→ 主语前加 by；双宾语可把其中一个提前。不及物动词（happen, take place, appear, disappear）无被动。

在复合句中修饰名词或代词的从句；被修饰的名词叫先行词，引导从句的词叫关系词。

关系词先行词成分例句
who人主/宾The boy who is reading is my brother.
whom人宾语（正式）The doctor whom she saw is famous.
which物主/宾The book which is on the desk is mine.
that人或物主/宾This is the most exciting film that I've ever seen.
whose人的/物的定语I know a boy whose father is a pilot.
  • 关系词作宾语时可以省略：The man (who/that) you met is our teacher.
  • 只能用 that 的情况：先行词是 all, everything, nothing, anything 等不定代词；被序数词或最高级修饰；先行词既有人又有物；先行词被 the only, the very 修饰。
  • 非限制性定语从句（逗号隔开）不用 that，用 which/who。

构词法

  • 合成词：class+room→classroom；black+board；motherland；warm-hearted；good-looking。
  • 派生：前缀 un-/im-/in-/dis-（happy→unhappy, possible→impossible, like→dislike）；re-（rewrite）；后缀 -er/-or（worker, actor）、-tion（invention）、-ment（development）、-ness（happiness）、-ful（careful）、-less（careless）、-ly（carefully）、-y（sunny）、-able（enjoyable）、-ous（dangerous）。
  • 转化：water n. 水 → v. 浇水；colour n. → v.。

冠词

  • a/an（首次提到、泛指一类、每一个）：a university, an hour, an honest boy, a one-way ticket（看音素不看字母）。
  • the（特指、上文提过、世上唯一、序数词/最高级前、the+adj 表一类人 the rich）。
  • 零冠词：球类运动、三餐、学科、by+交通工具、复数泛指、专有名词；但 play the piano（乐器加 the）。

九年级下册Modules 1–8（中考综合）

  • 关系副词：when（先行词时间，在从句中作时间状语）、where（地点，作地点状语）、why（原因 reason）。
  • 关系代词与关系副词选择看成分：从句缺主/宾语用 which/that；从句不缺主干成分、只缺状语用 when/where。

I'll never forget the day when we met. This is the school where I studied for three years. The reason why he was late is that he missed the bus.

状语从句

  • 时间：when, while, as soon as, until/till, before, after。
  • 条件：if, unless（除非 = if…not），as long as；遵守“主将从现”：主句将来时，if/unless/as soon as/when 引导的从句用一般现在时。
  • 原因：because, since, as；目的：so that；结果：so…that / such…that；让步：although/though（不与 but 连用）；比较：than, as…as。

时态结构标志词用法
一般现在时do/does；be(am/is/are)every day, often, usually, always, on Sundays习惯、客观事实、真理
一般过去时did；was/wereyesterday, last week, …ago, just now, in 2010过去发生的动作或状态
一般将来时will do；be going to dotomorrow, next year, soon, in the future将来动作/预测/打算
现在进行时am/is/are doingnow, look, listen, at present此刻或现阶段正在进行
过去进行时was/were doingat 8 last night, this time yesterday, while过去某时刻正在进行
现在完成时have/has donealready, yet, ever, never, just, since, for, so far过去动作影响现在；持续到现在
过去完成时had doneby last year, before/by the time+过去时过去的过去（拓展）
过去将来时would do；was/were going to do宾语从句中（主句过去时）从过去看将要发生（拓展）
第三人称单数现在时：动词加 -s/-es（watch→watches, study→studies）；主谓一致：either of/each of/everyone 作主语谓语用单数。

建议信

结尾

In a word / All in all / In conclusion, … / I'm looking forward to your reply. / Let's take action from now on!
